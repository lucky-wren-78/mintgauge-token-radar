How a value is built — model, holdout test, exclusion audit

The model

Out-of-sample test (printed, not promised)

25 repeated 80/20 holdouts on the 128 traded protocols. A miss of ×3.55 means the typical estimate lands within a factor of 3.55 of the real market cap. The flat multiple is what a single TVL multiplier scores on the same splits.

Why the bands are wide

Each row's band is the interquartile range of cross-fitted model residuals — it includes real residual dispersion, not just parameter wobble. Bootstrap-only bands (about ×2.2 wide) would look tidier and be wrong; the holdout says the honest spread is this one. The wider P10–P90 range is in each protocol's detail view.
