    Every term, tested — the full ablation

    One term at a time on top of the base model, judged on leave-one-out and walk-forward together. Full per-category fits passed both tests on the median but were not adopted: the random holdout worsens (×3.67 vs ×3.55) and the leave-one-out error tail explodes (max ×2,096 vs ×922) — thin categories overfit.
